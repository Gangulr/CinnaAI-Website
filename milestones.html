12-month plan

From requirement analysis to the final report.

Month 1

Requirement analysis

Background study, literature review, define objectives

Month 2

System architecture

IoT, ML and robotics architecture diagrams

Month 3

IoT chamber prototype

Light control, temperature and humidity control

Month 4

Multispectral camera setup

NDVI sensors, image capture pipeline

Month 5

Dataset collection

Cinnamon greenhouse images and sensor logs

Month 6

ML preprocessing

Image cleaning, dataset labelling, augmentation

Month 7

Disease detection model

CNN and multispectral classifier

Month 8

Growth prediction model

LSTM forecasting model

Month 9

Robot design

Robotic arm 3D model and mechanism development

Month 10

Robotic peeling prototype

Integration of motors, blades and sensors

Month 11

System integration

IoT, AI and robot merged, cloud dashboard

Month 12

Testing and final report

Accuracy testing, evaluation, dissertation writing
